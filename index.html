GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

We're building a clear, dependable experience from the first click.

Is GoodLife ready for real payments? +

This downloadable version is a front-end demo. Real payments require a secure backend, verified payment and VTU provider accounts, server-side verification, and production testing.

Can I fund my wallet? +

The demo does not collect money or create real balances. A payment gateway and verified webhook flow must be configured before launch.

How do rewards and referrals work? +

Rewards should follow published eligibility rules. A production backend must calculate commissions and prevent self-referrals, duplicate claims, and abuse.

How do I get support? +

Add a monitored support email or WhatsApp Business channel before launch. Never send passwords, PINs, or payment credentials to support.
